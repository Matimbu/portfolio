// Who I Am

About Me

I'm Raywel Francis T. Martin, an IT student at STI College Malolos, from Bulacan. I'm still early in learning to code, and it sticks best when I build something and work out where I get stuck — also known on the court as Yao Ming — The Court's Coder.

Most of my time has gone into The Hive, a self-order kiosk written in C# and WinForms. I didn't use a UI library, so I drew the buttons, tabs, and product tiles myself with GDI+ — partly a design choice, partly because I wanted to understand how the rendering actually worked. Building it alone meant working through the interface, the order flow, and the payment logic one problem at a time. There's still plenty I'd do differently now.

I built this site in plain HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap, so I pay attention to how things look as well as whether they run. I'd rather be clear about what I know well and what I'm still learning. Right now I'm looking for OJT and junior developer roles, and I'm glad to be pointed at things worth learning next.

// System Project

The Hive Kiosk

"One Screen. Order to Receipt."

A real self-order kiosk built for The Hive Cafe — every button, tab, and animation is hand-painted on raw GDI+, running inside a single window from welcome screen to printed receipt.

  • No pop-ups — one shell window
  • Custom UI, no third-party library
  • 46-item menu, 7 categories
  • Cash, card & e-wallet checkout

Project Background

Started from a bare WinForms template and built out into a full point-of-sale flow — menu, cart, payment, and a till-style receipt — as my first solo project.
