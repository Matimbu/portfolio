# v2.1: The contact form sends messages

**Released:** October 5, 2026
**Previous version:** [v2.0](v2.0.md)

## Changed

- **The message form in Contact now sends directly** through Formspree, straight to my email. In v2.0 it opened the visitor's own email app instead.
- Visitors see "Sending...", then a thank-you once it's delivered. If it fails, they get a message telling them to try again or email me directly.
- The spam trap from v2.0 stays: a hidden field that people never see and bots fill in.

## Also

- The repository was recreated on October 5 with the same name and address, so the full history and the `v1.0` and `v2.0` tags carried over unchanged.
